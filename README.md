# Fallout Content Pack

## Description:
The Fallout Content Pack is a Barotrauma mod which aims to make a full conversion.

### What is currently added?

#### Weapons:
**Laser Weapons** 

* Laser Pistol
* Laser Rifle
* Laser Rifle (Assualt) - Fully Automatic
* Laser Rifle (Heavy) - Powerful Laser Sniper
* Laser Musket

**Plasma Weapons**

* Plasma Pistol
* Plasma Rifle
* Plasma Sniper

**Special Weapons**
* FatMan


#### Armour:

* Combat Armour
* Reinforced Combat Armour MK1
* Reinforced Combat Armour MK2

* T-45 Power Armour

#### Clothing:
* Enclave Officer uniform
* Brotherhood of Steel Elder/Scribe uniform


### What will be added and changed?
* Power Armours will have 2 types, Outer Body and Exosuit. Outer body Power Armour will use the armours from the older games before 4, exosuit power armours will be an external suit like the base game exosuit. Both types will have their own pros and cons.
* Vault-Tec equipment
* NCR equipment
* Enclave Equipment
* Brotherhood of Steel Equipment
* New afflictions
* Medical
* Consumables
* Factions
* Dynamic Europa support due to the extensive amount of factions that will be present.
* Standalone items with no factional changes for players who **JUST** want Fallout items.
* Voice Modulation for power armour and other suitable equipment.


### Medical:
* Stimpak:
  * Heals most external afflictions
* Doctors bag:
  * Heals blunt force damage on limbs
* Buffout:
  * Increases the users melee damage and slightly increase damage resistance
* Med-X:
  * Increases damage resistence between 0.30 - 0.65
* Psycho:
  * Increases melee damage
  * Damage resistence
  * Speed
  * Skin colour tint
  * Organ damage





### Factional:
#### Vault-Tec
* Vault-Tec Jumpsuit
  * Standard apperal which will have minimal bonuses.
* Vault-Tec Mechanic Jumpsuit
  * Gives bonuses to mechanical engineering skill. (Will work on repair failure damage modifier)
* Vault-Tec Engineer Jumpsuit
  * Gives bonuses to electrical engineering skill. (Will work on zapped/electrical damage modifier)
* Vault-Tec Scientist Jumpsuit
  * Gives bonuses to medical skill and has 3 additional inventory slots for chems and medical items.
* Vault-Tec Overseer Jumpsuit
  * Gives bonuses to helm skill and will be unique.
 
#### Brotherhood of Steel
* Initiate
  * Lowest rank of the BoS, minimum equipment.
* Scribe Engineer + Veteran
* Scribe Mechanic + Veteran
* Scribe Elder
* Knight
  * Portable power armour (Weaker than exosuit power armour)
* Paladin
  * T-60 exosuit power armour
* Sentinel
  * Captain of submarines
* Elder
  * Outpost managers (Will use either old school elder apparel or overcoat model)

#### NCR
* NCR Trooper
  * Standard security
* NCR Rangers
  * Stronger equipment, higher skills
* NCR Veteran Rangers
  * Rare best NCR equipment, very high skills
* NCR Heavy Trooper
  * NCR salvaged power armour, slower but still keeping the defence stats as other power armours. + HMG
* NCR Engineer
* NCR Mechanic
* NCR Captain
  * Captain of submarines
* NCR General
  * Outpost managers
